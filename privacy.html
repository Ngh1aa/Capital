What the prototype does

Navigation, local page interactions and planning forms run in the browser. When you choose to prepare a leasing email, the browser opens your configured email application using a mailto: handoff.

What the prototype does not claim

It does not claim to provide a production CRM submission, confirmed booking, live vacancy database, authenticated tenant service or payment flow.

External services

Links to the official Capital Place website, maps or other first-party documents leave this prototype and are governed by the destination service’s own policies.
