# Capital Place — Visual Signature v3

Date: 2026-09-01

## Strongest cues to preserve

- Real Capital Place architecture and workplace photography.
- Graphite / cream / white base with restrained orange decision accents.
- Large editorial typography and architectural scale.
- Twin-tower / floorplate / workplace planning information.
- Thang Long-inspired architecture story only where supported by official content.
- Calm, high-trust leasing language; no marketplace urgency treatment.

## Digital signature cues

### 1. Orange locator
Use orange as a locator, state marker, focus indicator, active navigation rule and primary-action accent. Do not wash whole sections in orange simply to increase brand presence.

### 2. Planar architectural geometry
Use square or near-square panels, thin 1px rules and aligned edges. Components should feel closer to an architectural specification, directory or leasing document than to rounded SaaS cards.

### 3. Building / page indexing
Long decision pages may use numbered section indexing inspired by physical building directories and elevation/floor references. This is an L1–L2 domain metaphor: structural, not skeuomorphic.

### 4. Technical proof rhythm
Specs, capacity, certification and availability information should use strong baseline/grid alignment, compact labels and scan-friendly numerical hierarchy.

### 5. Cinematic-to-practical sequence
Lead with genuine building/place imagery; transition into diagrams, floor plans, specification data and conversion controls as user commitment increases.

## Generic category patterns to avoid

- Centered heading + three identical cards repeated section after section.
- Excessive rounded cards, pills, glass panels or floating app controls.
- Black-and-gold generic luxury property styling.
- Decorative blueprint textures or fake CAD drawings.
- Motion that hides practical leasing information.
- Stock-office imagery that can be mistaken for Capital Place.
- Bright marketplace badges for representative availability.

## Correct application

- Home hero: real building at landmark scale; simple verified facts.
- Office: floor plan and capacity are primary decision objects.
- Availability: status language is explicit and restrained; orange indicates action/selection, not false urgency.
- Location: visual city context plus meaningful travel/business relationships.
- Sustainability: certification + measurable evidence + occupier relevance.
- Mobile: flatten the same grammar into full-width sections; do not shrink desktop directory rails.

## Incorrect application

- Turning every number into an orange card.
- Using dragon-scale motifs on buttons, forms and every container.
- Adding paper/blueprint textures to simulate an architect desk.
- Applying luxury-gold gradients because the asset is premium Grade A.
- Treating representative floor data as a real-time listing marketplace.

## Recognition QA checklist

Remove the logo/header/footer from representative screenshots and check:

- Does graphite/cream/orange behavior remain consistent?
- Are planar rules and numbered indexing visible without becoming repetitive?
- Does photography clearly belong to Capital Place?
- Do specs/floor plans feel integrated with the editorial visual system?
- Is the primary leasing action distinguishable without multiple competing accents?
- Does mobile retain the same design DNA without preserving desktop-only decoration?
- Are focus, selected and error states visible without depending on color alone?

## Status

This is a proposed digital visual-signature system derived from verified/preserved project assets and public Capital Place evidence. It is not represented as an official corporate brand guideline.
