# Capital Place — Design Reference Benchmark V6

Checked: 2026-09-01

## Project decision
- Domain: Grade A commercial office / landmark building.
- Audience: tenant decision-makers, workplace/real-estate teams, brokers, visitors.
- Business goal: qualified leasing enquiry / private viewing.
- Brand constraints: Capital Place official logo/assets, graphite/white/stone with restrained orange wayfinding.
- Reality constraint: no verified live inventory/pricing/CRM in the prototype.

## Extracted design DNA

### Layout grammar
- architectural split-screen rather than generic centered hero.
- major media and technical object can occupy 60–70% of a section.
- use asymmetric editorial spreads when content roles differ.
- use floor-plan/elevation as primary information surfaces.

### Hierarchy
- landmark statement first.
- building facts immediately visible.
- practical decision objects before decorative gallery content.
- conversion follows proof, not the reverse.

### Color/surface
- dark architecture zones for identity/proof.
- warm stone/white for planning/specification clarity.
- orange acts as datum/wayfinding/CTA.

### Imagery
- real building/workplace photography dominates.
- image crops communicate scale, façade identity, workplace quality, or city context.

### Interaction
- straightforward links/forms and optional reveal motion.
- no novelty interaction that hides navigation or leasing actions.

### Conversion/trust
- distinguish published reference information from current availability.
- current floors, divisibility, timing and terms must be confirmed by Leasing.
- static prototype must not imply CRM/booking success.

## Rejected patterns
- SaaS dashboard/card language for availability.
- universal centered heading + 3-card section template.
- glassmorphism/gradient as generic premium styling.
- black/gold luxury aesthetic not supported by Capital Place identity.
- fake live vacancy labels or pricing.
- copying a reference site's signature geometry or branded campaign language.
