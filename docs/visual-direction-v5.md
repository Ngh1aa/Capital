# Capital Place Visual Direction v5

Date: 2026-09-01

## Why v5 exists

The previous QA-focused pass improved structural correctness but did not create a sufficiently visible visual redesign. V5 changes the art direction and page composition while preserving verified Capital Place content, routes and leasing reality.

## Direction

**Architectural editorial × blueprint precision × building-directory wayfinding**

Capital Place should feel like a premium Grade A building and leasing presentation, not a SaaS dashboard, generic corporate template or marketplace.

## Reference synthesis

### Capital Place official website — brand and property truth
Keep:
- graphite / white / restrained orange behavior;
- real architecture, workplace and amenity photography;
- Thang Long / dragon-scale identity as cultural story;
- Grade A, dual LEED, office scale and leasing facts.

Do not copy:
- current page composition or legacy navigation literally;
- weak contrast or any visual state that hides the logo/CTA.

### The Spiral — spatial storytelling principle
Extract:
- architecture first;
- large spatial imagery;
- clear sequence from building story to workplace, amenities and availability.

Adaptation:
- use Capital Place real imagery and floor-plan assets;
- keep availability truthful as leasing-confirmation rather than live inventory.

### 22 Bishopsgate — building-as-place principle
Extract:
- building facts remain highly scannable;
- workplace/amenity narrative is separate from leasing state;
- strong information hierarchy without turning the site into a property marketplace.

## Domain-artifact synthesis

Dominant real-world artifacts:
1. **Building podium / elevation** — used in navigation composition and vertical hierarchy.
2. **Blueprint / floor plan** — used for section rules, grids, floor planning and availability layouts.
3. **Building directory** — used as wide-screen in-page orientation.

Fidelity: L1–L2. The site borrows structure, numbering and datum-line behavior, not fake paper/blueprint textures or literal skeuomorphism.

## Visual signature

1. **Dark podium brand block** at the left of navigation, separated by a 4px orange datum line.
2. **White navigation deck after scroll** while the podium remains dark, keeping the white official logo legible.
3. **Orange is an action/locator color**, not a decorative fill everywhere.
4. **Large architectural headings** with compact uppercase metadata and short orange datum rules.
5. **Asymmetric 38/62, 42/58 and 58/42 grids** rather than centered heading + cards.
6. **Flat square controls**, thin construction lines and no rounded SaaS chrome.
7. **Dark architectural footer** with orange top datum.

## Page grammar

### Home
Monumental hero → building scale → anatomy/elevation → architecture → workplace/floor-plan → leasing action.

### Location
City hero → map + dark editorial panel → city route field → business/diplomacy → metro future → neighbourhood/workday → arrival.

### Office
Office hero → building anatomy → measurable scale → floor plan/test fit → requirement finder → tower stack → workplace experience → leasing.

### Amenities
Workday itinerary → The Link → The Nexus → meeting/hosting → focus → hospitality → wellbeing → B1 directory.

### Sustainability
Credential proof → evidence grid → building systems → façade/water → human benefit → tenant value → community.

### Availability / Space
Requirement → tower/floor structure → reference match → floor-plan detail → compare/save → request viewing. It must read as a leasing decision tool, never as a live marketplace.

## Color roles

- `#211D1D` — primary architectural ink / podium / dark sections.
- `#F3F0E9` — warm paper / blueprint editorial field.
- `#FFFFFF` — primary reading surface.
- `#F15F22` — primary action, active state and locator datum only.

## Responsive behavior

- Desktop navigation is split into podium + navigation deck.
- Under 1150px primary nav becomes a menu; podium remains visible.
- Multi-column architectural compositions collapse to one deliberate vertical flow under 900px.
- Mobile headings recompose independently; they are not simply scaled desktop headings.
- Mobile menu uses a subtle construction grid and large readable links.

## Do not

- do not place the white logo on a white navigation surface;
- do not use outline-only primary CTAs with low-contrast text;
- do not add generic rounded cards, glass pills or dashboard chrome;
- do not invent live vacancy, pricing, availability or commercial terms;
- do not use orange as a large decorative background across every section;
- do not make every page use the exact same section template.
