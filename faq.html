What type of office building is Capital Place?

Capital Place is positioned as a Grade A office development with two 37-storey towers, approximately 93,000 square metres of net leasable area and an amenities floor for retail.

Where is Capital Place located?

Capital Place is at 29 Lieu Giai, Ngoc Ha, Hanoi, in an established commercial and diplomatic precinct. See Location for the wider city context.

What are the building operating hours?

Tenants with authorized access can operate 24/7. Standard office hours are 8:00 AM–6:00 PM Monday–Friday and 8:00 AM–12:00 PM on Saturdays. After-hours operations require prior registration with Building Management.

What office spaces are currently available?

Live vacancy, divisibility, handover timing and commercial terms are not published as an unverified online list. Use Availability to frame a requirement or ask Leasing to confirm the current schedule.

Can a floor be subdivided?

Divisibility is confirmed for the specific floor and requirement by Leasing. Public floor plates on this website are planning references, not a live unit inventory or binding offer.

How can I arrange a private viewing?

Request a viewing through the website or contact Leasing directly. The team will assist with available spaces, viewing arrangements, floor plans and relevant leasing information. A requested time is not confirmed until the team replies.

What should I prepare before visiting?

Have your host company, tower, meeting contact and appointment details ready for reception. Office and tenant-only amenity access may require host approval or visitor registration. See Visit for the arrival sequence.

Is parking available?

Parking access and availability should be confirmed with your host or building contact before arrival. Follow on-site signage and security direction.

What amenities are available?

Public references include landscaped common areas, F&B, a business hub with coworking, event and lounge areas, and a fully equipped gym. Explore Amenities and The Link directory for the current public overview.

Are current opening hours listed online?

Current opening hours for individual brands are available on request. The directory is a public reference and should not be read as a live operating-hours schedule.

Does the building support tenant ESG goals?

Capital Place presents sustainability, internationally recognised green-building standards, efficient operations and wellbeing-focused features as part of the building story. Certification details and ESG documentation are available through Leasing; see Sustainability for the public overview.

Are there community and sustainability initiatives?

Public examples include wellness initiatives, annual blood donation drives, first aid training, Green Station, seasonal events, networking and tenant engagement programmes.
