Ask Leasing
What type of office building is Capital Place?

Capital Place is a Grade A office development with two 37-storey towers and approximately 93,000 sqm of leasable area.

Where is Capital Place located?

Capital Place is at 29 Lieu Giai, Ngoc Ha, Hanoi, within an established business and diplomatic district.

What office spaces are currently available?

This website does not publish an unverified live vacancy list. Use Availability to frame your requirement and ask Leasing to confirm the current schedule.

Can a floor be subdivided?

Divisibility is confirmed for the specific floor and requirement by Leasing. Public floor plates are planning references rather than a binding unit list.

How can I arrange a private viewing?

Send a leasing enquiry or contact the team directly. A requested time is not confirmed until the team replies.

What should I prepare before visiting?

Have your host company, tower, meeting contact and appointment details ready for reception. Tenant-only areas may require host approval or visitor registration.

Is parking available?

Parking access and availability should be confirmed with your host or building contact before arrival. Follow on-site signage and security direction.

What amenities are available?

Capital Place publicly presents The Link at B1, shared work/meeting settings at The Nexus, hospitality spaces, dining, fitness and other everyday services.

Does Capital Place support tenant ESG goals?

Capital Place is dual LEED-certified and publishes building-performance and sustainability evidence. Detailed certification and ESG documents are available through Leasing.

Are opening hours and commercial terms listed online?

Individual brand opening hours and current commercial leasing terms are not presented as a real-time online source. Confirm them with the relevant building or leasing contact.
